\# Simple Pancake Recipe



\## Ingredients



\- 150 g plain flour

\- 2 eggs

\- 300 ml milk

\- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

\- A pinch of salt

\- Butter for cooking



\## Instructions



1\. Put the flour and salt into a bowl.

2\. Add the eggs and milk, then whisk until smooth.

3\. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and add a little butter.

4\. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.

5\. Cook for 1–2 minutes until golden, then flip.

6\. Cook the other side for another minute.

7\. Serve warm with honey, fruit, or syrup.



\## Preparation Time



\- Preparation: 5 minutes

\- Cooking: 10 minutes

\- Servings: 4



\## Tips



\- Add a little more milk if the batter is too thick.

\- Serve with fresh berries or sliced bananas for extra flavour.
